Quiet confidence on a near-black field. Glass panels catch a single specular edge, teal carries the signal, and monospace runs through everything as texture. Nothing glows unless it means something.

01Principles
Glass over voidSurfaces are translucent layers over #08080c — directional borders, a lit top edge, one specular corner. Depth without shadow theater.
Color means somethingTeal is the signal. Amber and crimson are categorical labels, never decoration, and crimson never touches a metric.
Mono as textureJetBrains Mono carries labels, status, and metadata at wide tracking. It is a design material, not a code affordance.
Motion is reactiveThings move because you touched them. Decelerate in, accelerate out, stagger reveals — nothing loops at rest.

05Motion

Three moves cover the whole system: things enter by decelerating, respond in under 200ms, and signal with one concentric pulse. Everything animates transform and opacity only — glass never changes size or blur mid-flight — and nothing loops at rest.

tokenvalueuse
ease-enter400ms · cubic-bezier(0.16, 1, 0.3, 1)reveals, sheets, cards in
ease-exit220ms · cubic-bezier(0.7, 0, 0.84, 0)dismiss, collapse
micro180ms · ease-outhover, focus, toggles, press
stagger45ms per item · cap 8list + grid entrances
pulse900ms · ease-enter · oncethe signal ripple — saves, syncs, go-live
sweep700ms · ease-out · hoverspecular edge crossing a card

Decelerate in, accelerate out — exits run faster than entrances, because leaving should feel lighter than arriving. Past the eighth item a stagger reads as lag: cap the cascade and land the rest together. Reduced-motion collapses everything to instant state changes.

06Voice

Plain, present tense, quietly certain. Interfaces state what happened and what to do next. "Saved. Synced to two devices." — never an exclamation point doing the work a checkmark already did.

07Do / Don't

Do

  • Light the top edge — glass reads from its borders
  • Track mono labels wide; let them breathe
  • Keep density high and spacing disciplined
  • Use teal for the one thing that matters now
  • Step crimson to its ink the moment it carries words
  • Simulate glass when backdrop-filter isn't safe

    • Don't

  • No pure white — #DCDCE0 is the ceiling
  • No crimson on metrics — it reads as loss
  • No Syne for numbers — it's a display face
  • No accent cycling for decoration
  • No looping animation at rest
  • No light mode — the void is the canvas
