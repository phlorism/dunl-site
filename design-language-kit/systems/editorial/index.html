Newsreader set against a working monospace. One restrained ink blue. Structure carried by typography — rules, weights, and optical sizes doing what boxes and shadows usually fake.

Design language · No. 03 Reading measure 66ch v1.0
01 · Principles

Two typefaces in permanent conversation: the serif reads, the mono annotates. Every folio line, caption, byline, and datum is JetBrains Mono in small caps tracking — the machinery of the page kept visibly separate from its prose. The page is a column, not a grid; the measure holds at 66 characters and everything else negotiates around it.

The blue is spent like a byline budget. It appears on links, one pull-quote rule, the drop cap, and the primary action — and nowhere else. When everything is neutral, one color is a headline.

05 · Motion

A page of prose earns stillness. Motion lives at the edges — the arrival of a page, the turn to the next, the quiet color change of a link. Mid-column, while the reader is reading, nothing moves. Ever.

tokenvalueuse
settle300ms · ease-outpage and section entrances — opacity + 8px rise
ink180ms · easelink + button color turns — color only, nothing else
rule-draw500ms · ease-in-out · oncethe heavy rule draws left to right on arrival
turn240ms out · 300ms inpage-to-page crossfade — old ink dries before new ink lands
none0mswhile reading — the column never moves

Everything is opacity and a few pixels of rise. No parallax, no scroll-jacking, nothing follows the cursor. Reduced-motion prints the page instantly.

Color is the only hover: a link turns its ink in 180ms, and underlines never thicken or slide.

If a hover changes anything but color, it is too loud for this room.

--motion-ink:     180ms ease;         /* color only */
--motion-settle:  300ms ease-out;     /* opacity + 8px rise */
--motion-rule:    500ms ease-in-out;  /* scaleX, origin left, once */
--motion-turn:    240ms out · 300ms in;
--motion-reading: 0ms;                /* the column never moves */
06 · Voice

Complete sentences, unhurried, precise. Interface copy reads like a well-edited caption: "Saved to your library. Filed under Craft." The mono register handles facts; the serif handles meaning. Neither exclaims.

07 · Do / Don't

Do

  • Hold the measure at 60–70ch, whatever the viewport
  • Let optical sizing carry the display weight
  • Keep every folio, caption, and datum in mono
  • Spend the blue like a budget — links + one action
  • Separate with rules; a 3px rule is a headline
  • Use white panels only where data needs a desk

    • Don't

  • No boxes around prose — typography is the structure
  • No second accent, ever
  • No serif below 14px — that's the mono's floor
  • No justified text; ragged right reads honest
  • No border-radius above 0 — corners are square
  • No motion while the reader is mid-column
