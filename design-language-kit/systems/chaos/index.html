Every card on this page just drew a lot: a neon from the pool, a tilt between one and three degrees, a radius from {0, 6, 14, 24}. In five seconds it draws again. That's the whole system — tendencies, not rules, and exactly one rule anyway.

01Principles
Seeded, not sloppy

Randomness is drawn from pools someone curated. Wrong-on-purpose still has to compose — alive, not ugly.

Clash the type

Six fonts, zero harmony, one page. Hierarchy survives on size and color because family loyalty is dead.

Everything tilts

Nothing sits square. One to three degrees is enough to feel hand-placed; more is a mess, and mess is banned.

One rule keeps

Blue #3D00FF can't spell — 2.5:1 on black. It stays a fill; words in blue use blue-ink #825BFF. Chaos, but legible chaos.

05Motion

Three registers: the interval (the whole page redraws itself every five seconds), the slam (entrances hit hard, once, then hold still), and the jitter (RGB split on hover — steps(2), never smooth). Easing between lots would imply the lots were related.

The chrome never joins in — controls hold stable green while the content rolls. Reduced-motion kills the interval, the marquee, and the jitter — the page freezes on its first roll and stays honest.

06Voice

Loud, lowercase-or-ALLCAPS, self-aware. "saved. probably." — the interface mocks polish but never the person. Errors are blunt and useful: "that broke. here's the undo."

07Do / Don't

Do

  • Draw every lot from a curated pool
  • Cap tilt at 3° — hand-placed, not dropped
  • Keep body copy in mono at full white
  • Freeze everything under reduced-motion
  • Keep system chrome on stable green
  • Step blue to blue-ink the instant it spells

    • Don't

  • No blue words, no blue borders — fills only
  • No re-rolling mid-read on inputs or chrome
  • No random type sizes — clash faces, not scales
  • No muted below #888888 — 5:1 is the floor
  • No two solid neons touching
  • No ugly. Alive is the brief; ugly is a miss
