Design language · No. 05

The pace of a
well-made book.

Warm cream, bronze, frosted glass. Depth comes from light and blur alone — no shadows, no elevation stack, nothing in a hurry.

luxuryboutiqueconcierge surfacesv1.0
01 · Principles

Light is the depth

Frosted panels float over soft bronze pools. Blur separates layers; there is no drop shadow anywhere in the system.

One metal

Bronze does everything an accent must — and steps to its ink, #7B664B, the moment it spells a word.

Wide-set restraint

Small caps track at 0.32em. Generosity in spacing is the luxury; the type never raises its voice.

Unhurried motion

Every transition takes 350ms or longer. Nothing snaps. The interface turns pages; it doesn't switch screens.

05 · Motion

Nothing here is urgent. The interface turns pages rather than switching screens: 350ms is the floor, exits trail like a held door, and the only permanent motion is light — the pools breathe on marketing surfaces and nowhere else.

tokenvalueuse
turn350ms · ease-in-outevery state change — the floor, not the ceiling
drift700ms · ease-outpanels arriving, light pools settling
veil500ms · easescrims and frosted sheets — blur fades in with the glass
breathe9s · ease-in-out · loopambient light only, marketing surfaces only

If a transition feels efficient, slow it down. Nothing bounces, nothing springs — momentum is for machines. Reduced-motion turns pages instantly and stills the pools.

06 · Voice

Gracious and specific — the concierge register. "Your table is held until 8:20. The cellar list is attached." No urgency, no exclamation, no upsell in the confirmation. Errors take responsibility quietly: "That date is fully committed — the 14th and 15th remain."

07 · Do / Don't

Do

  • Float glass over light — blur is the elevation
  • Track the small caps wide and keep them small
  • Fill standard buttons with bronze-ink
  • Give every transition at least 350ms
  • Let cream and paper alternate as the rhythm
  • Ship the paper fallback where blur isn't supported

    • Don't

  • No drop shadows — light and blur only
  • No bronze sentences — that's bronze-ink's job
  • No second accent; one metal per house
  • No upright Instrument — the italic is the mark
  • No motion under 350ms — nothing here is urgent
  • No pure white, no pure black — cream and ink
