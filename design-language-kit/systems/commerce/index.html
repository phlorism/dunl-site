01 · Principles

Whitespace is the container

No boxes doing the work air can do. Sections separate by scale and space; borders are the last resort.

One ask per viewport

Every scroll position has exactly one pop CTA. If two things glow, neither is the point.

Pop is a fill, not an ink

Neon teal at 1.9:1 can't spell. Buttons fill with pop and label in ink; teal words use pop-ink.

Serif is the wink

Instrument italic appears once per story — the emphasis beat inside a Jakarta headline, never body copy.

05 · Motion

One orchestrated moment when the page opens; after that, motion only answers the shopper's hand. Hovers lift, presses compress, reveals ride the scroll — and the cart is the one place allowed a spring, because it's the moment worth celebrating.

Motion sells continuity, not spectacle. Never animate a price — it must read as fact, not fireworks. Nothing moves that the shopper didn't cause, except the hero, exactly once.

06 · Voice

Benefit first, plain verbs, zero jargon. Buttons say what happens: "Start free trial", and the confirmation keeps the same words — "Trial started. First charge on Aug 12." Prices never hide; the fine print is short enough to read.

07 · Do / Don't

Do

  • Lead with the story; the product close behind
  • Give every viewport exactly one pop CTA
  • Label pop fills in ink, always
  • Fill standard rose buttons with rose-ink
  • Let whitespace do the separating
  • Spend the italic once per story

    • Don't

  • No white text on pop — 2.0:1, the old bug
  • No teal sentences — that's pop-ink's job
  • No competing CTAs in one view
  • No borders where a margin would do
  • No serif body copy — the wink is not a voice
  • No countdown-timer urgency theater
