Design language · No. 06 · The Undergrowth beneath the Woodlands

Made for small hands
and big wonderings.

Storybook display over a clean reader face. Seasonal light, Totoro-pace motion, and two guides — Reed the fox and Cove the wolf pup — who never rush anyone.

kidslearningfield guidesv1.0
01 · Principles

Light tells the time

The canvas shifts with season and hour — spring morning cream to afternoon green-white. The palette moves; the layout never does.

Soft edges, honest depth

Rounded everything, bark-tinted shadows at 12%, borders you could trace with a finger. Nothing sharp near small hands.

The guide never rushes

Motion is floaty and slow — the Totoro pace. Success celebrates gently; mistakes are just the next try.

Montessori bones

One task per screen, real progress over points, and an exit a grown-up can always find. The interface is furniture, not a fairground.

05 · Motion · the Totoro pace

Motion in the Woodland is weather, not machinery. Ambient things float on a seven-second breath, arriving things settle with one soft overshoot — like landing on moss — and success blooms once, gently. Nothing near a lesson moves faster than 300ms, and nothing ever hurries a child.

tokenvalueuse
float7s · ease-in-out · loopambient drift — hills, clouds, Cove's tail

hop450ms · settle curveReed & Cove reactions — one hop, never a loop
bloom900ms · oncesuccess moments — pollen, never confetti storms
daylight900ms · easetime-of-day canvas shifts

Slow enough to watch, soft enough to ignore. One overshoot per landing — a second bounce is a fairground. Reduced-motion stills the forest and keeps every lesson identical.

06 · Voice

Short sentences, second person, always forward. "Not that one — good try. Which sound starts moon?" Reed asks; Cove wonders aloud; the interface never says wrong, failed, or time's up. Grown-up copy drops the characters and keeps the warmth.

07 · Do / Don't

Do

  • Re-light the palette by season; keep every role fixed
  • Keep touch targets 48px+ and edges soft
  • Step leaf to its ink whenever it spells a word
  • Celebrate small — one bloom, then back to the trail
  • Keep typing and settings behind the grown-up gate
  • Let Fraunces tell and DM Sans teach

    • Don't

  • No blossom or pollen sentences — decoration can't spell
  • No timers, streaks, or anything that punishes pausing
  • No hard shadows or pure black — bark tints only
  • No motion faster than 300ms near a lesson
  • No more than one task per screen
  • No character voice in parent-facing copy
