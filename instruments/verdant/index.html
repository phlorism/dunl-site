VERDANT

An orchestra grows here.

THE SONGWRITER

Every seed writes one song. A hook is composed first — chord-tone anchors on the strong beats, one deliberate leap, stepwise falls, an asking phrase answered by a resolving one — then the form is built around it the way pop builds around a chorus: intro, verse, pre-chorus climb, and the hook arriving in full sun, twice, before a night bridge and a final chorus lifted two semitones. The score in the corner is the actual hook, as written. The chorus is a promise; the countdown tells you when it's kept.

THE ORCHESTRA

Everything is synthesized live from raw oscillators and shaped noise — no samples. Detuned string sections with slow bows and spiccato chops, French horns and a chorus trumpet, flute and clarinet trading the verse, harp glissandi at the seams, celesta sparkle an octave up, timpani rolls under the build, and a pocket of soft claps, shaker and sub pulse that keeps the whole thing moving like a song, not a soundtrack. The groove swings; the strings breathe; the lift is earned.

THE MEADOW

The landscape performs the form: dawn for the intro, morning verses, full-sun choruses, dusk falling across the bridge, and dawn again — brighter — for the lifted final chorus. Birds answer the melody. Flowers bloom where notes land, colored by pitch, on the same circle-of-fifths wheel as everything else here. Notes you play are remembered: the meadow keeps your favorite scale colors and lets the harp and celesta ornament with them.
